# Retail Sales Analysis | Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview
Performed an end-to-end Retail Sales Analysis using a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and revenue patterns. The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Excel:** Data cleaning, data validation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and initial analysis.
- **PostgreSQL:** Data querying, aggregation, joins, subqueries, CTEs, window functions, and business analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.

## Key Activities
- Cleaned and prepared raw sales data by handling missing values, duplicates, inconsistent entries, and data quality issues.
- Performed exploratory data analysis to understand sales performance and customer demographics.
- Analysed total revenue, order volume, quantity sold, and average order value.
- Evaluated revenue contribution across product categories.
- Compared purchasing patterns between male and female customers.
- Identified top-performing products and categories.
- Examined sales trends over time and customer purchasing behaviour.
- Developed SQL queries to answer real-world business questions and generate actionable insights.

## Business Objectives
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute most to overall sales?
- How do purchasing patterns differ by gender?
- What are the monthly sales trends?
- Which customer segments contribute most to revenue?
- How can sales performance and business decisions be improved?

## Project Outcome
The analysis provides insights into revenue distribution, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and sales trends, helping businesses understand their customers and identify opportunities for growth.
